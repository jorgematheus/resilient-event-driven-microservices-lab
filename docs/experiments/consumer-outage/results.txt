Experiment: Consumer Outage and Queue Backlog Recovery

Date: 2026-09-30

Queue: payment-queue

Scenario:
The Payment Service consumer was temporarily unavailable while
messages remained in the SQS queue.

Initial backlog:
Approximately 100 messages.

Observed recovery:
~100 messages
~80 messages
~20 messages
0 messages

Result:
After the Payment Service consumer was restored, the accumulated
messages were progressively processed and the queue returned to
zero messages.

Conclusion:
The experiment demonstrated asynchronous buffering and backlog
recovery through the SQS-based communication path.
